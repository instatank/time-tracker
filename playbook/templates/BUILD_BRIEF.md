# BUILD BRIEF — [project or feature name]

*Fill this before starting any new project, or any major new feature on a Tier-1 project
(NORTH_STAR.md §3). Ten minutes, in plain English. A Claude session can interview you through
it — just say "walk me through a build brief." Save the finished brief in the target repo's
root. If a line is hard to fill, that's the brief working: hard-to-fill means not ready
to build.*

## 1. User & pain — one sentence each
- Who is this for? (Be honest if it's "me." If it's not you: have they told you they want it,
  or are you guessing?)
- What specific moment of pain does it remove?

## 2. Why this, why now — the training answer
- Which track does this stretch (P / S / D / O — see NORTH_STAR.md §2), and what will building
  it teach that the last build didn't?
- If the honest answer is "nothing new — I just want it," that's allowed, but label it: this
  is a *comfort build*, budget it small, and don't confuse it with progress.

## 3. Smallest shippable slice
- What is the version that is usable in ONE session of building? (Not the vision — the slice.)
- What is explicitly NOT in v1? (List 3+ things you're tempted to include.)
